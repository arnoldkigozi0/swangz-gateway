**The swap** follows `glass-login.mp4` (30fps; stills 48–60): the old words fade out in 4 frames (140ms), the glass is bare for frames 4–5, the new words fade in over frames 6–12 (190ms delay, 200ms), the two planes and the card settle over 570ms. The old and new words are never visible together. Reduced motion: everything instant. `signin.cjs` pauses the animations and reads opacity at each 1/30s frame, so the laptop's slow rendering cannot change the answer.

**The reveal (font and Google).** Nothing in the gate (card, brand, footer) is painted (`visibility:hidden`, which layout-shift scoring ignores) until the server's answer to `/auth/options` AND the Inter typeface are in, or 1.2s has passed, whichever is first; the hold also runs when a page passes no answer (the Netlify console). The request and its handling are unchanged.
- *Typeface.* The default is the system sans (`"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`). At the reveal, Inter is latched on once (`.xg-inter`) only if every face was loaded by then. A font that arrives later, or never (2.5s, 404, hung), is ignored for the life of the view, so no text is re-wrapped after the reveal. Tag: **10.1.3** (a performance fallback: on a slow or missing font the sign-in is set in the system sans instead of Inter).
- *Google.* The 94px a Google button and divider take is held (`.xg-late`) only if the answer was still outstanding at the reveal. A late "yes" fills that room in place (exactly one correct link, nothing moves). A late "no" (off, refused, or hung then dropped) leaves a quiet gap that is handed back at the next B to A swap, while sign-in A is hidden. An answer that already said no (and only the font was slow) holds nothing: the box is 0px. Tag: **10.1.3 / 10.1.4** (a layout difference only in the late-and-off case).
- *Google while on Get access.* On a phone the card is only as tall as the pane showing, so a late "yes" that lands while Get access (staff, mode B) is showing would grow that pane and move the whole gate. The staff link in B is held back (`.xg-hold-b`, `display: none`) until the next swap, and shows with that swap; the Login side gets its link in the room held at the reveal. Covered by `lateGoogleOnBChecks` in `signin.cjs` (staff, 1440x852 and 390x844). Written from the source and not run in a browser when committed: see the PR for the CI result. Tag: **10.1.4** (the link in B appears one swap later than the answer).
- *Measured* (`signin.cjs`, both apps, 1440x852 and 390x844, 12 cells each: fast, Inter 800ms, Inter 2.5s, Inter 404, Inter hung, off and refused with a 2.5s font, answer at 1500ms on/off/refused, hung, refused). Every layout-shift entry is recorded with its sources (text nodes and null nodes counted as inside), plus the reveal time from a MutationObserver. Result in every cell: reveal 174-1221ms after the mount (the 1.2s cap plus timer jitter), zero entries inside `.xgate` after the reveal (not a threshold: none), the title, both fields and the Login button move less than 1px, raw global total 0.000000, and no horizontal overflow on a phone. Negative control: with Inter forced in while the font is 2.5s late, the same recording reports entries (0.000015 staff, 0.000045 console), so the check can see a swap. The earlier fix (commit 9dc063a) reproduced 0.004959 on 5d184f6 with a late font and a refused answer.
- *Phone form width.* On a phone the form pane now stretches to the card (`align-self: stretch; min-width: 0`), not to the width its input would like: staff had been 6px wider than its card (362 against 356) and console exactly fitted. Found by the new overflow check, not a visual change on desktop.
- *Not claimed.* Probe's raw overlays (`/tmp/probe-s57/out`) put the 1440x680 still against an 852px capture without moving the card corner, so they show a 30px card offset that is the framing, not the product; they are not committed here and nothing here claims the reference acceptance, which is Probe's gate.
