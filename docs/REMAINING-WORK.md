## 14-day feasibility (target: 16 November 2026)

A **limited internal beta** centered on the existing Gateway/PWA and a controlled desktop cohort is
plausible if G2 is prompt, G3/G4 close, and CI verification uses hosted Windows/macOS runners. A
Linux standalone app already exists. Unsigned Windows/macOS test builds are plausible to produce
through the manual CI workflow, but they are not production installers.

Signed Windows and macOS installers are **not a committed 14-day outcome**: signing certificates,
Apple Developer ID/notarization access, protected CI configuration and review time have not been
verified. If Arnold confirms the required accounts/certificates are available promptly, the team can
re-estimate after G2; no purchase or account change is assumed. A native Android/iOS app with signed
builds and real-device testing is not realistic within this window and is not required to beta the
existing installable PWA. Full cross-platform app integrity/self-update is also a release gate: until
G3/G4 pass, constrain any beta to a reviewed distribution method and make its limitations explicit.

No production rollout, data import, merge, DNS change or deployment is included in this matrix.
